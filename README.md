# One-Liner Laugh Lab — The Sock Defense

A single-line comedy entry for the [One-Liner Laugh Lab](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroso-69-one-liner-laugh-lab-6ccf3aae) bounty on make.fun. The prompt asks for the excuse a robot vacuum gives after eating a sock, and this entry answers it as a deadpan legal defense: the vacuum pleads that anything left on the floor is, by its own house rules, legally dust — then rests its case, and the sock. The joke lands on a fresh angle (a courtroom defense from a household appliance) with a double twist at the end, kept tight at 118 characters with no wasted setup.
